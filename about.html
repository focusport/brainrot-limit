About Brainrot

Attention is the thing worth protecting.

We build tools that give you control over your own attention without making you give up the parts of your phone that make life easier.

Why we built it

Every screen time tool we tried had the same flaw: it punished the whole app. Set a 30-minute timer on Instagram and it also locks the DMs you need to answer. Set one on YouTube and it cuts off the lecture you are halfway through. So you tap "ignore limit", and the moment you do, the feed is one swipe away.

The problem was never the app. It was one specific, endless, algorithmic scroll inside it. Brainrot was built to recognise that scroll and limit only that.

Brainrot: Reel Limit was previously known as FocusPort Limit. Same app, same team, a name that says what it fights.

What we believe

  • Rules beat willpower. A decision made in the morning is more reliable than one made at 11pm.
  • Targeted beats total. Limits last when they don't get in the way of real life.
  • Your data is yours. A tool about your attention shouldn't be harvesting it.
  • Encouragement over guilt. A small mascot is a gentler nudge than a red warning.

What we won't do

  • Sell or share your usage data
  • Show ads inside the app
  • Require an account
  • Read your messages or what you type

A note on honesty

Brainrot is a self-control tool. It isn't a medical product and doesn't diagnose or treat anything. If screen use is affecting your sleep, mood or relationships, talking to a doctor or counsellor is worth doing alongside any app.

Try it for a week.

Free on Google Play. If it isn't for you, uninstalling removes everything.
