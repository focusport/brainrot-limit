Features

Everything Brainrot does, in one place.

A short-video limiter, a screen time controller, and a few small tools that make both easier to stick to. This is what each part does.

Get it on Google PlayHow it works
01 — SHORT-VIDEO LIMITER

Per-video counting for four feeds.

Brainrot has a dedicated detector for each feed, so it can tell a Reel from a regular post and a Short from a long video.

  • Instagram Reels: counts swipes in the Reels viewer. DMs, Stories and the regular feed stay open.
  • YouTube Shorts: counts Shorts only. Lectures, podcasts and music play without a counter.
  • Facebook Reels: stops the autoplay chain of vertical videos.
  • Snapchat Spotlight: caps the endless Spotlight feed while chats and Snaps stay available.
02 — LIMIT MODES

One shared budget, or one per app.

Unified gives you a single daily pool. Watch 20 on Instagram and 30 on YouTube and a 50-video limit is used up. It's the mode that stops you from swapping one feed for another.

Individual lets you set separate numbers, say 20 on Instagram, 10 on YouTube and 0 on Snapchat, for when each app plays a different role in your life.

03 — FOCUS SESSIONS

Timed sessions with no way out.

Start a focus timer with quick presets or schedule recurring sessions. While a session runs, distracting apps are blocked and notifications are muted. Only the essential apps you chose, such as Phone and Messages, stay available.

  • Focus timer from 15 minutes upward
  • Recurring schedules by day and time
  • Essential-apps allow list
  • Emergency access if something real comes up
04 — INSIGHTS

See whether it's working.

A 7-day usage history, weekly total and daily average for your short-video watching, plus focus time, completed sessions and a streak for your focus sessions. Everything is computed on your phone.

Also included

The small things.

None of these is the headline, but together they are why the app stays out of your way.

Floating counter

Appears only inside a short-video feed and disappears when you leave it.

Any-app time limits

Set a daily time limit on any installed app: games, news, shopping, anything.

11-stage brain mascot

From fresh at 0 to completely fried at 1000+. A number you can read at a glance.

Hard stop screen

When you hit the limit the feed closes. There is no snooze button to bargain with.

Essentials stay open

Calls, messages, maps and the apps you choose are never blocked.

No account, no ads

Install and go. Data stays on the device and is deleted when you uninstall.
