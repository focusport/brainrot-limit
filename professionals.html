For professionals

Protect deep work from the micro-break.

A thirty-second check of a feed rarely costs thirty seconds. Research on interruptions suggests it can take well over twenty minutes to get fully back into demanding work. Brainrot keeps the phone break a break.

Why it works for work

You keep your phone. You lose the loop.

Messages, calendar, banking and maps still work. The one thing that goes is the part designed to be hard to leave.

Between tasks

Breaks with an end.

During a build, a render or a long meeting prep, a small daily allowance gives you the break without the rabbit hole.

Deep-work blocks

Scheduled focus sessions.

Schedule 90-minute blocks. Distracting apps are blocked, and Phone and Messages stay available for real interruptions.

Privacy

Nothing leaves the device.

No account, no cloud sync and no analytics. Safe to run on a personal phone you also use for work.

A WORKDAY SETUP

Three settings that cover most of it.

  • Unified limit of 30 short videos a day across every feed
  • Focus schedule for your two best deep-work windows
  • App limits of 20–30 minutes on news or shopping apps that fill the gaps
